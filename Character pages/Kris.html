KRIS DREEMURR

Kris is the playable protagonist of DELTARUNE and the only known human living in Hometown alongside a community of monsters. They are quiet, odd, and difficult to read known around town for pranks and eccentric behavior. Kris lives with their mother Toriel and attends Hometown's school, where they share a class with Susie.

What makes Kris unique is the uneasy tension between the player's control and their own will. At several key moments, Kris acts entirely independently ripping the SOUL from their chest at the end of chapters, creating Dark Fountains alone at night, and displaying emotions the player never chose.

PERSONALITY

Kris is described by townsfolk as strange, quiet, and prone to odd behavior. They are known for eating chalk, sneaking around at night, and making unsettling jokes. Despite this, those who know them well Toriel, Noelle, and Asriel clearly care for them deeply, suggesting warmth beneath the surface that Kris rarely shows openly.

Their relationship with the player is one of the game's central tensions. Kris never speaks of their own volition all choices are the player's. Yet they act alone the moment the player loses control, suggesting a rich inner life entirely separate from whoever inhabits them.

ROLE IN THE STORY

Kris serves as the central figure in DELTARUNE, embodying the conflict between player agency and character autonomy. Their journey is one of self discovery and the struggle to maintain their identity while navigating the complexities of their existence in a world where they are both human and something more.

TRIVIA

· "Kris" is an anagram of "risk," fitting their role as a character whose intentions are perpetually uncertain.

· Kris is the only human in a town populated entirely by monsters.

· Toriel mentions Kris used to be afraid of the dark as a child giving their nighttime activities a deeply ironic quality.

· The red SOUL represents DETERMINATION the same trait that drives the player in UNDERTALE.

· Kris's solo scenes always happen after Toriel puts the SOUL to bed, suggesting Kris is most themselves when the player isn't watching.
